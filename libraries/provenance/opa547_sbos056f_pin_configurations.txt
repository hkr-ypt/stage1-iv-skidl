EXTRACT     : pdftotext -layout -f 2 -l 2 (poppler 24.02), the lines of the
              "PIN CONFIGURATIONS" drawing copied verbatim below. This data
              sheet has no pin table: the drawing numbers the leads
              "1 2 3 4 5 6 7" left to right and labels each lead with a
              leader line, the labels staggered over two text rows so that
              they fit. The leader lines are graphics and do not survive
              text extraction; the labels' left-to-right positions do.
USE         : U5 is fitted with an OPA547T in the symbol and footprint that
              were authored for the OPA548T. tools/author_project_symbols.py
              reads each package's labels below in left-to-right order as
              leads 1..7 and requires every lead to carry the same function
              as the same-numbered pin of the OPA548 "Pin Functions" table
              (opa548_sbos070d_pin_functions.txt), which the symbol is
              already checked against. A disagreement stops the build.
              Figure 1 of the same data sheet prints the same lead numbers
              on the amplifier symbol (V+ 5, V- 4, inputs 1 and 2, output 6,
              ILIM 3, E/S 7); that figure is not parsed.
